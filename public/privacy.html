Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

jplus2 is a code editor for the Mac made by Jason Leiton. It does not collect any data about you.

What jplus2 does not do

jplus2 has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send your files, your code, or any information about how you use the app to the developer or to anyone else.

Your files

jplus2 opens only the files and folders you choose. They stay on your Mac. To reopen your previous session, jplus2 keeps a list of those files, and a copy of unsaved text, in its own private folder on your Mac.

jplus2 Lite

jplus2 Lite has no network features. It never connects to the internet by itself; the Help menu only opens this website, an email message or the donation page in your own browser when you choose them.

Services you connect

jplus2 talks to other services only when you set them up and ask for something:

  • Databases. If you add a database connection, your queries go directly from your Mac to the server you entered.
  • GitHub. If you connect a GitHub account, jplus2 uses it to list your repositories and show their folders and files, to create and merge pull requests, to clone, push and pull, and to create gists when you ask. Files you open from GitHub are saved as temporary copies on your Mac.
  • AI models. If you connect a model, the question you type, and the file and selection you chose to include, are sent to that model's provider (for example Anthropic) when you press Send. That provider's own terms and privacy policy apply to what it receives.

Connection details are stored on your Mac, and passwords, tokens and API keys are stored in the macOS Keychain. None of this passes through the developer of jplus2.

This website

This website uses no cookies. It counts visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly service that does not use cookies, does not track you with unique identifiers and keeps only aggregate totals, such as which pages are visited, which site sent the visitor and the type of browser or device, not your IP address. It does not run if your browser sends Do Not Track. The language you pick with the EN | ES switch is remembered in your own browser and is not sent anywhere. The free jplus2 Lite installer is hosted on GitHub, which counts how many times it is downloaded; the author sees only that total. GitHub's own privacy statement applies while you download it. If you enter your email to download jplus2 Lite or to join the waiting list for the full version, it is sent to Buttondown, the service we use to send jplus2 news, and kept there until you unsubscribe or ask for it to be deleted. We use it only for jplus2 news, such as the release of the full version, and every message has an unsubscribe link. To have your email deleted, write to leiton@live.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
