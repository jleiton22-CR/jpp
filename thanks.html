Install it in three steps

  1. Open the file

    Double-click J++-Lite-1.0.0.dmg in your Downloads folder.

  2. Drag it to Applications

    In the window that opens, drag J++ Lite onto the Applications folder.

  3. Open J++ Lite

    Open it from Applications or Launchpad. The first time, macOS may say it cannot check the app: open System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security, find the message about J++ Lite and press Open Anyway.
