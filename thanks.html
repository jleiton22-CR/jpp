Install it in three steps

  1. Open the file

    Double-click jplus2-Lite-1.0.1.dmg in your Downloads folder.

  2. Drag it to Applications

    In the window that opens, drag jplus2 Lite onto the Applications folder.

    Open it from Applications or Launchpad. The first time, macOS may say it cannot check the app: open System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security, find the message about jplus2 Lite and press Open Anyway.
